MODEL NOTES /

What these estimates mean

What is modeled?

Payment impacts are estimated using 2024 Medicare utilization held constant and a modeled 2026 Medicare baseline. The 2027 phase-in uses the finalized fee schedule model, geographic PE weighting, and the finalized Modifier 25 reduction assumptions.

What is hypothetical?

The fully implemented PE scenario is an unstabilized long-run illustration. Hypothetical LN2 zero-day conversion assumes 17000, 17004, 17110, and 17111 convert to 000-day globals. None, min, mid, and max correspond to removing 0%, 20%, 50%, or 80% of a geographically adjusted 99212 value from those four codes, respectively. Add-on code 17003 remains ZZZ and receives no direct modeled RVU reduction. These conversions are not included in the CY 2027 final rule.

How does the slider work?

The default reproduces the precomputed physician or aggregate result. Moving it scales the Modifier 25 dollar component by the ratio of the selected to modeled same-day rate. It does not rebuild encounter combinations or alter CF, RVU, or MPPR; it is an illustrative sensitivity calculation.

What is uncertain?

Physician utilization is historical and certain claims-level combinations are approximated. The geographic factors and ordinary-MPPR reranking are modeled estimates. The site does not predict changes to service volume, site of care, coding, G2211 usage, or future CMS/RUC decisions.

Important: This is an independent policy modeling tool, not an official CMS payment calculator. Figures estimate Medicare Physician Fee Schedule reimbursement for the included professional services, not all Medicare Part B payments or total practice revenue. HCPCS A-, J-, and Q-code items were excluded. Hypothetical scenarios are not forecasts or enacted policy. Results may differ from actual paid claims.
